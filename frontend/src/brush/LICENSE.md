# Brush stroke data

`glyphs.json` holds stroke outlines and stroke paths for the kanji and kana that
`BrushKanji.jsx` writes on screen. It is generated by `scripts/brush.mjs` from
[AnimCJK](https://github.com/parsimonhi/animCJK), copyright 2016-2026 FM and SH.

- Kanji strokes derive from the Arphic PL KaitiM fonts and the Make Me a Hanzi
  project, and are redistributed under the Arphic Public License. See
  `licenses/ARPHIC-PUBLIC-LICENSE.txt`.
- Kana strokes are redistributed under the GNU Lesser General Public License,
  version 3 or later. See `licenses/LGPL.txt`.

`licenses/ANIMCJK-COPYING.txt` is AnimCJK's own notice. Anyone who redistributes
`glyphs.json` must keep these notices with it and pass on the same rights.
