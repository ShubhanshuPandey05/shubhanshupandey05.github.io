Scope: `/` (home), `/work`, `/blog`, `/blog/:slug`, `/resume`, the 404 page, and a matching restyle of `/admin/*`.
Visitor mode: Experience on the public routes, Operate on the admin.

Audience and job: hiring managers for backend roles first, contract clients second. They need proof of shipped systems, the resume, and a way to write, all within seconds.
Proof on hand: three deployed projects with public URLs and source; GitHub, LinkedIn, LeetCode; resume PDF. No screenshots, no portrait, no testimonials.
Constraints: content stays CMS-driven; every admin function keeps working; existing routes keep working; the page must look complete with zero images.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The portfolio is a mountain farmhouse at dusk that the visitor walks through. The house is what you see; the work is what holds it up. It refuses the dark hero over a row of project cards.

OWN-WORLD: Committed indigo. Soot-dark cedar is the ground and the structure (beams, posts and sills are the layout rules). Indigo-dyed cotton owns whole fields. Lit shoji paper is the only reading surface. Vermilion lacquer for torii, sun, seals and postbox; blossom pink for petals only. One kumiko lattice module sets columns and gaps. Kanji are brush-written in true stroke order; Latin display is an ink-bled mincho; a Japanese gothic for reading. Objects: noren, paper lanterns, andon lamps, tansu drawers, hanging signs, a round postbox.

STORY: The visitor sees a name on a noren and lanterns already lit: products live right now. They pass the cloth, sit on the engawa (who he is), enter the workroom (each project an andon lamp, lit because its URL answered), open the tool chest, and leave by the gate where the postbox stands. They believe he ships things that run. They open a live product, read the resume, or write.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A cedar beam across the top carries navigation and Resume. Under it a five-panel indigo noren fills the upper two thirds; the name is dyed in white across the left panels at display scale with the role beneath; a large brush kanji pair sits on the right panel. To the right of the cloth, paper lanterns hang from the eave, one per live project, each clickable. Below the hem the valley shows: terraces, a vermilion torii, a cherry tree shedding petals, the red sun on the ridge. Primary action: the lanterns, and See the work at the hem.

FORM: Kominka farmhouse walk-through, candidate 5 of 7 on the ordered list, seed key 35eeb6db. Signature interaction: the noren parts as you pass and lanterns light only when a project's URL responds. Motion grammar: cloth sway, sliding shoji, lamp ignition, falling petals, the sun setting with the scroll so the visit runs from dusk into night.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
